# Pricing and monetization proposal

These are initial hypotheses for operator review, not configured prices, market-validated recommendations or projected revenue. Live paywalls use store-localized product metadata. Offer the same clearly described features on each purchase surface and verify any policy constraints before changing links or checkout placement.

| App | Proposed monthly USD | Proposed annual USD | Free value | Paid reason to return |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| Rehearsal Room | 8.99 | 49.99 | Two guided scenarios, retry and saved practice | More situations, optional responsive AI and transcription |
| Care Relay | 3.99 | 24.99 | One circle and essential task handoffs | Multiple circles and reusable templates |
| Meal Patch | 4.99 | 29.99 | Constrained additions and ten saved meals | More saves, weekly plan and ingredient list |
| Quote to Cash | 12.99 | 79.99 | Three quotes per month and approvals | Repeat client work and reusable scope templates |

Do not make an annual purchase the default without an equally clear monthly option. Show the actual billing period and total price. If using a trial, show eligibility, duration, renewal price and cancellation route as returned/confirmed by the purchase provider. Do not claim “free” when a purchase is charged immediately.

Rehearsal AI usage is bounded at 50 turns and 15 transcriptions per account per day; monitor actual provider costs and abusive traffic before widening limits. Measure contribution margin from actual receipts less refunds, store/payment fees, AI, hosting and support. Do not assume gross subscription price is profit. A spreadsheet is not needed to begin: preserve dated provider exports and calculate cohort costs consistently.

For HAMM, demonstrate why paid features match repeated value while essential free tasks remain useful. The portfolio currently implements subscriptions, not one-time packs or consumable credits. Do not claim a diverse revenue stack that has not been built. Add another purchase model only after users show a different need and it can be tested within the deadline.

Grand priority: grow genuine paying-user adoption efficiently. Pick one audience/channel with a credible path to repeat use. Run a small, authorized acquisition test only after activation and purchase work; any advertising budget requires a separate decision. Reinvest based on observed conversion and retention, not the size of a possible hackathon prize.
