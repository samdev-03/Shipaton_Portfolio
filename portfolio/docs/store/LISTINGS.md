**Full description**

The real conversation starts here.

Rehearsal Room gives you a private place to find your next sentence before a conversation at work. Choose a situation, respond in your own words, and try the moment again with one small adjustment.

Start with guided practice for setting workload boundaries and speaking up about credit. Read supportive wording cues, hear a response aloud, and reflect on how ready you feel. Your saved practice keeps the next step close.

With Rehearsal Room Pro, explore more scenarios, including asking for a raise, giving feedback, interviewing and agreeing on scope. Choose responsive AI practice or voice transcription when you want it. AI processing requires your permission; guided practice remains available without it.

There is no score for your worth or promise of a workplace outcome. Feedback offers informal wording suggestions so you can decide what sounds right for you. Avoid entering confidential work material.

Optional reminders are scheduled by you. Analytics and AI processing have separate controls. Export or delete your account data in Settings.

| Apple promotional text | Make the next helpful step visible. A shared circle shows who can help, who has acknowledged a task, and what is complete. |
| Apple keywords | care,tasks,circle,help,community,organize,handoff,shared,household |
| Suggested primary category | Productivity |

**Full description**

One next step. Together.

Care Relay helps a small trusted group organize everyday help. Create a private circle, share a one-use invitation, and add a task with the details someone needs to act.

Members can claim a task, acknowledge it, and mark it complete. When plans change, hand the task to another member; they acknowledge the handoff before it is considered accepted. Clear status words keep the shared list understandable.

One circle and the essential shared-task workflow are free. Care Relay Pro adds multiple circles and reusable task templates for recurring needs. Optional personal reminders help you return at a time you choose.

Only circle members can read its tasks and member display names. Invite people you trust, and share only information you have permission to share. The list updates when you refresh; this is not an emergency alert or clinical care service. Do not enter medical records or children's personal information.

You control optional analytics and reminders. Export or delete your account in Settings. Deleting a circle owner's account deletes their circles for every member; save anything you need first.

Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription. Available plans, billing periods and localized prices are shown before purchase. Manage or cancel through the purchase provider. Deleting an account does not cancel a subscription. Designed for adults.

You do not have to start over to make a meal your own.

**Full description**

Less back-and-forth. A clearer next job.

Quote to Cash helps independent home cleaners prepare a quote that customers can understand. Add your business and customer names, describe included work and exclusions, and itemize the price. Set the tax rate, deposit percentage and valid-until date that apply to your quote.

Share an approval link with the intended customer. They can review the quote and record acceptance with their name. Accepted terms stay fixed. Revoke a link if it was shared with the wrong person, and schedule a personal follow-up reminder when useful.

Create three quotes per month free. Quote to Cash Pro adds unlimited quotes and reusable scope templates for repeat work.

The app records quote approval; it does not collect the customer's service payment, issue invoices, keep accounting books or determine your tax obligations. Arrange payment directly with your customer. A typed approval is not identity verification or a guarantee of an enforceable contract. Anyone holding a valid approval link can read and accept the quote, so share it carefully.

Optional analytics and reminders have separate controls. Export or delete your account data in Settings.
