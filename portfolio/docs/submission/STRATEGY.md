This plan cannot force store review or create a long growth history in the remaining days. Newly affected Google personal accounts may have testing gates that prevent a from-zero launch in a week; review your account's eligibility immediately. Use an already eligible store account if available. The Next Gen-only route can preserve a qualifying student's entry, but not the other categories.

For Grand, devote post-release time to user acquisition and retention rather than more features: choose one narrow audience, make a usable first session easy, remove observed friction, and measure voluntary purchases. App revenue, customer quote approvals and service-payment volume are different measures. Keep a dated RevenueCat export as the financial source of truth. No fabricated transactions, self-purchase loops, testimonials, engagement or reviews.

Do not claim Kotlin Multiplatform, ads, Noise or Galaxy-specific optimization for this Expo implementation. Those would require distinct implementation and operational evidence; adding labels would not make it eligible.
