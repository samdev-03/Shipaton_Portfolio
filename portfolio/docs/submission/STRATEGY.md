**Career Coaching — Leadership Heather:** Rehearsal Room helps new managers actively practice setting workload boundaries, giving feedback and making a contribution visible. A user enters a response in a realistic scenario, receives a short wording cue, retries the same moment with a different sentence, and saves a reflection. Guided scenarios work without sending text to an AI provider; adults can opt into responsive AI practice and voice transcription. Before-and-after readiness is the user's self-report, not a claim of measured career benefit. The product does not use the influencer's likeness or claim an endorsement.

**RevenueCat Design:** Please inspect the focused retry interaction, readable conversation layout, restrained color palette, scenario cards and reflection flow. Retry replaces the subsequent branch so the user can explore one changed sentence without restarting. Feedback remains short and actionable, while visible keyboard dismissal and a recoverable microphone-denial path keep the small-screen conversation usable. The recorded native walkthrough and original screenshots show the implementation; no custom animation or accessibility result beyond the supplied evidence is claimed.

**RevenueCat Peace Prize:** The intended benefit is a low-pressure place for early-career professionals and new managers to practice difficult conversations before a real meeting. This is a feasible working product, but the social-benefit hypothesis has not yet been validated by a user study. The next step is observing consenting users and learning whether they leave with a useful next sentence.
